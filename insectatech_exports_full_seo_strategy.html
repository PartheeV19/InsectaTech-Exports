Priority 1 Fix JS rendering — enable SSR or pre-rendering
Your entire site content is invisible to Googlebot because it's rendered by JavaScript. This single issue blocks all other SEO efforts. Everything else you do will have zero impact until this is fixed.
If your site is Next.js: switch pages from CSR to getServerSideProps or getStaticProps. If it's plain React: integrate Prerender.io or use a static-site generator. Ask your developer to verify using Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool — it shows exactly what Google sees.
Priority 2 Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
Create /sitemap.xml listing every page (home, about, all 6 category pages, each product page, contact). Verify ownership in GSC and submit. This tells Google exactly what to crawl.
Go to search.google.com/search-console → Add Property → insectatechexports.com → Sitemaps → Submit sitemap URL
Priority 3 Add JSON-LD schema markup on every page
Add Organization schema on homepage, Product schema on product pages, and BreadcrumbList on all inner pages. This enables rich results and business knowledge panels.
Minimum schema for homepage: Organization (name, url, logo, address, sameAs LinkedIn/IndiaMART). For each product page: Product (name, description, offers, image). Test at search.google.com/test/rich-results
Priority 4 Canonical tags + robots.txt
Add a robots.txt at insectatechexports.com/robots.txt and canonical tags on every page to prevent duplicate content issues between www and non-www versions.
Add <link rel="canonical" href="https://insectatechexports.com/page/"> in the <head> of every page. In robots.txt, add: Sitemap: https://insectatechexports.com/sitemap.xml
Priority 5 Page speed & Core Web Vitals
Run PageSpeed Insights on your homepage. Target LCP under 2.5s and CLS under 0.1. Compress images to WebP format, use lazy loading, and minimize unused JavaScript bundles.
Test at: pagespeed.web.dev — share the score with your developer and ask them to address the top 3 recommendations.
